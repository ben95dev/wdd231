Project Site Plan

Retail Hub

Site Name

Name: Retail Hub

Selection Reason: This name clearly identifies the platform as a centralized digital catalog and service point for everyday retail inventory, customer inquiries, and local product availability.

Optional Domain Availability: retailhub-local.org

Site Purpose

The site serves as a digital storefront and customer portal. It aims to display current in-stock retail inventory, highlight featured discounts, provide contact details, and allow customers to submit inquiries or product requests online before visiting the physical shop.

Scenarios

  • Scenario 1: "Does this shop currently have [specific product/item] in stock, and what is the current price?"
  • Scenario 2: "What are the physical shop's operating hours and contact options for bulk orders?"

Color Scheme

The site plan uses a primary navy hue for structure and hierarchy, paired with a warm amber for call-to-action highlights and clear visual contrast.

Primary: #1E3A8A Header backgrounds, primary buttons, and main section headings.
Secondary: #D97706 Accent highlights, hover states, and call-to-action borders.
Dark Neutral: #1F2937 Body text and main content readable markup.
Light Neutral: #F3F4F6 Main page background and content card containers.

Typography

Headings Font: Montserrat (Sans-serif) — Used for all section titles (`h1`, `h2`, `h3`) to create a bold, clean structure.

Body Text Font: Open Sans (Sans-serif) — Used for paragraphs, lists, and form elements to ensure clear, accessible readability across mobile and desktop devices.
